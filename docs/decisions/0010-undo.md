# 0010 — One undo history for the whole notebook

**Status:** accepted, 2026-10-26. The owner chose Maddox's own history
(option 1) and the recommendation on the three questions below.

## Problem

Ctrl+Z was MathLive's undo, one history per cell. Deleting a cell threw
its history away and moved the caret to the cell above, so Ctrl+Z then
undid something there. A deleted cell could not be brought back.

The roadmap item was "Move and recover cells": reorder with
Alt+Shift+↑/↓, undo a deleted cell, Ctrl+↑ to recall the previous input.
The owner dropped reordering and Ctrl+↑, and asked for undo to be "the
last action", not per cell.

## Options considered

- **Maddox's own history** (chosen). Maddox handles Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y /
  Ctrl+Shift+Z in every cell; MathLive's undo is never reached there.
- **Thread MathLive's per-cell histories into one order**, listening to
  its `undo-state-change` events and asking the right cell to undo.
  Rejected: MathLive decides how typing is grouped, and our own edits
  (bracket deletion, matrix rows, refilled boxes) go around its history,
  so the two orders could drift apart.

## Decision

1. **One list of steps, newest first**, whichever cell they were in: a
   change to a cell's text, a new cell (Ctrl+Enter, or Enter on the last
   one), a deleted cell, and Alt+Enter. Undo moves the caret to the change.
   A new change after undoing drops what could be redone.
2. **Typing comes back a word at a time.** A run of letters, digits and
   `.` is one step, and so is a run of deletions; an operator is a step of
   its own. A pause over a second, moving the caret (arrows, Home/End,
   Tab, a click) or another cell starts a new step (`history.ts`).
3. **A restored cell comes back exactly as it was**, results and graphs
   included (its element is re-attached). If it defines something, it
   runs again and the cells below that use it recalculate, the reverse of
   deleting it (0007).
4. **Undone text is not run.** The cell shows as changed, as after typing;
   Enter runs it.
5. **Cells only.** The graph panel's rows keep MathLive's per-row undo;
   settings and the angle mode are not in the history. The history is not
   saved, and starts empty when a notebook is opened or created.
